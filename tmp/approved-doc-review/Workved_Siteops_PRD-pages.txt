1. Product VisionCreate a centralized, WhatsApp-first platform that gives Workved reliable control over commercial interior project execution, clearly assigns responsibility, distributes the Site Supervisor's workload, tracks external approvals, and provides management with timely visibility into progress, delays, blockers, vendor performance, and internal support deployment.2. Business NeedProject status currently depends on calls, WhatsApp chats, spreadsheets, photographs, and verbal follow-up.The Site Supervisor carries excessive coordination, documentation, reporting, and follow-up responsibility.Tasks, support work, and external approvals do not always have one clearly accountable internal owner.Employee absence can leave work unowned or delay execution.Vendor commitments, delays, rework, and attendance are not consistently recorded.Management lacks one dependable source of truth for daily execution.3. 5. Core Operating ModelAdmin creates a project using the approved 45-day template.PM reviews generated tasks, owners, vendors, dependencies, and external approvals.Every active task and external approval receives one Primary Responsible Employee.Supporting employees may be attached for coordination or execution support without changing primary accountability.Employees and vendors receive assignments through WhatsApp.Employees submit progress and evidence; vendors only acknowledge or clarify assignments.Supervisor verifies work; PM approves applicable critical and quality-sensitive work.Absence triggers Reassignment Required; urgent work temporarily transfers to the Supervisor until reassigned.Daily and weekly reports are generated from recorded data.Management receives exception-focused visibility and decisions required.6. Core ModulesAuthentication and role-based accessEmployee, project-team, vendor, and sub-vendor managementMaster 45-day template and project generationTask and responsibility ownershipSupport employee allocationAbsence and mandatory reassignmentTask dependencies and external approvalsWhatsApp communicationSupervisor verification and PM approvalDelay, blocker, carry-forward, and no-update handlingDaily and weekly reportsManagement dashboardsAudit and Super Admin technical controls7. Non-Negotiable Business RulesEvery active task must have one Primary Responsible Employee.Every active external approval must have one internal owner.Supporting employees do not replace primary accountability.Employee absence must trigger reassignment; the system must not silently choose a replacement.Urgent unassigned work temporarily transfers to the Site Supervisor.Vendors cannot officially complete, verify, approve, or close tasks.The baseline schedule remains locked after activation; live changes retain history.Class A work requires Supervisor verification and PM approval before dependent work continues.Blocked and overdue are separate conditions and may apply simultaneously.Critical overrides require a reason and audit record.8.
