Privacy Policy

SiteOps ("the App") is an internal project-execution and coordination tool operated by Workved Spaces Private Limited ("Workved", "we", "us"). This policy describes how we handle information within the App, including through our use of the WhatsApp Business Platform.

Information we collect

We collect information necessary to operate the App for our team members, supervisors, project managers, and vendors, including: name, phone number, email address, employment/project role, and project- and task-related activity (status updates, approvals, attendance, evidence photos/documents submitted against a task).

How we use WhatsApp Business Platform data

Where a user has a registered phone number, the App may send and receive WhatsApp messages to notify users of project/task events (assignments, approvals, status changes, reminders) and to let authorized users take certain actions (such as accepting or verifying a task) by replying to a WhatsApp message. Message content and delivery metadata are stored to support this functionality and to maintain an audit trail of project decisions.

How we use this information

Information collected is used solely to operate project execution workflows within the App: assigning and tracking work, recording approvals and evidence, and notifying relevant team members and vendors of relevant changes. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Data retention

Project and task data is retained for the duration of a project and for a limited period afterward to support recordkeeping, after which evidence files are periodically deleted.

Data sharing

Information is shared only with other authorized users of the same organization/project (e.g. a Supervisor, Project Manager, or Admin who needs it to do their job), and with service providers who help us operate the App (e.g. cloud hosting, database, and messaging providers such as Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform), strictly to provide the App's functionality.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or wish to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at marketing@workved.com.

Last updated: 2026-09-07. This is an initial policy for an internal tool and may be updated as the App evolves.
